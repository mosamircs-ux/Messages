Privacy Policy

App: PulseSMS (Android package com.pulsesms.messenger)
Effective date: November 19, 2026

In short: PulseSMS reads and sends your text messages on your device. Your messages, contacts and settings stay on your device. The app does not have accounts, does not sell your data, and does not send your messages to the developer. The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob, which may use device identifiers as described below.

1. Who we are

PulseSMS is a free, open-source SMS and MMS messaging app for Android. This policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, and the choices you have. It applies to the PulseSMS app only.

2. Information the app accesses on your device

  • Text messages (SMS and MMS): read, send, receive, store and delete messages so the app can work as your messaging app.
  • Contacts: read your contacts to show names and photos for conversations and to help you pick recipients.
  • Phone state: used to identify the SIM/phone line for sending and receiving messages.
  • Notifications: used to show message notifications and quick actions such as reply and mark as read.
  • Calls: the app can start a phone call from a conversation when you choose to call a contact.
  • Settings and preferences: your theme, notification, blocking and other preferences, stored locally.

This information is processed on your device. PulseSMS does not upload your messages or contacts to any server operated by the developer.

3. Data stored on your device

Conversations, drafts, scheduled messages, blocked numbers and blocked keywords are stored locally on your device. You can delete conversations, empty the recycle bin, or uninstall the app to remove this data. You can also export your messages and blocked numbers to a file you choose; that file is saved wherever you direct it and is under your control.

4. Advertising and third parties

The version of PulseSMS distributed through Google Play displays banner ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may access the internet and collect device and usage information such as the advertising ID, IP address, device model, app interaction and approximate location derived from IP, in order to show and measure ads. This information is handled by Google under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services

You can limit ad personalization or reset your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings > Security & privacy > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads). The message content you send and receive is not shared with AdMob.

PulseSMS does not use analytics or crash-reporting services that transmit your personal messages, and it does not sell or rent personal information.

5. Permissions

PulseSMS asks for Android permissions only to provide the features described above. SMS, MMS, contacts, phone state, notifications and call permissions are used solely for messaging features. You can revoke any permission at any time in Android settings; some features will stop working if you do.

6. Children

PulseSMS is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Security

Because messages are stored on your device, their security depends on your device's security (screen lock, encryption and updates). PulseSMS is open source, so its code can be reviewed by anyone.

8. Your choices

  • Delete individual messages or whole conversations inside the app.
  • Revoke app permissions in Android settings.
  • Limit ad personalization or reset your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Uninstall PulseSMS to remove all data stored by the app.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed. Significant changes will be noted in the app's changelog.

10. Contact

For questions about this policy, open an issue at github.com/mosamircs-ux/Messages/issues.
